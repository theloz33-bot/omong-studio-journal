Disclosure and privacy

This page is short on purpose. It covers the three things a reader might reasonably want to know: whether links are paid, what the site collects, and who handles a purchase.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you subscribe to a tool through one of them, Omong Studio may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is the same either way.

Affiliate links carry rel="sponsored" and are labelled wherever they appear in a recommendation box. Tools are listed because they are used in the workflow the article describes, not because a commission is available — several tools in regular use here have no affiliate programme at all.

When an affiliate link is not yet available for a tool, the site links to the tool's official homepage instead. The link still carries rel="sponsored nofollow" so nothing about the page's intent is hidden from a reader or a search engine.

Purchases

Prompt packs, templates and other studio products are sold through Gumroad. The checkout, the payment, the receipt and the refund policy all belong to Gumroad; this site never sees or stores a card number. Any question about an order goes to Gumroad support with the order receipt.

Commissions

Video work commissioned from the studio is scoped and quoted in writing before anything starts, and the quote says what is delivered, when, and how many revision rounds are included. Any payment for commissioned work is handled between the client and the studio under the terms of that quote; this site takes no payment and stores no billing details. The three kinds of work and the pilot price are on the commission page.

What this site collects

No name, email address or phone number, and no first-party analytics.

  • No first-party analytics script, tracking pixel or session recording.
  • The site sets no cookies of its own. Advertising served by Google AdSense can, however, use cookies set by Google and other third-party vendors (including the DoubleClick cookie) to show ads based on prior visits. The cookie detail and the opt-out routes are in the privacy policy.
  • No newsletter form, no comment system, no login.
  • No third-party fonts or embeds; the page loads only its own CSS and icon.

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives standard request logs including IP addresses as part of serving a page. That logging is the host's, not this site's, and is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

If you write to the studio through a link on this site, the message goes to whichever service you chose to use, under that service's terms.

Corrections

If something published here is wrong — a workflow step that no longer works, a tool that changed its terms, a broken link — it gets corrected rather than quietly removed. The fastest route is the contact page, naming the page and what is wrong.

Commissions, orders and corrections go through the studio's public channels below. There is no contact form on this page, and no personal email address or phone number is published anywhere on the site.

## By purpose

### Video and music commissions

Long-form brand films, 9:16 ad cuts, original songs and vertical short-form series. The Korean marketplace storefront lists options and prices; message the store there. Work that is not listed on the marketplace is scoped by conversation as described on the [commission page](../services/), and the fee and schedule are fixed in writing before anything starts.

- Kmong storefront — [kmong.com/@오몽이](https://kmong.com/@%EC%98%A4%EB%AA%BD%EC%9D%B4)

### Digital products and refunds

Prompt packs, templates and single-file tools are sold on Gumroad. Checkout, receipts and refunds are handled by Gumroad; include the order number from your receipt when writing to them.

- Gumroad — [lozzy36.gumroad.com](https://lozzy36.gumroad.com/)

### Corrections and broken links

If something published here is wrong — a workflow step that no longer works, a tool that changed its terms, a dead link — it gets corrected rather than quietly removed. Open an issue on the public repository with the page address.

- GitHub — [theloz33-bot/omong-studio-journal](https://github.com/theloz33-bot/omong-studio-journal/issues)

### The Korean journal

Household and one-person production writing for Korean readers lives at [오몽 라이프 & AI 연구소](https://theloz33-bot.github.io/omong-kr-lab/), which has its own [contact page](https://theloz33-bot.github.io/omong-kr-lab/contact/). Both journals and the working channels are collected at the [studio hub](https://theloz33-bot.github.io/).

## Response time

Replies come within two to three business days. For a commission, include the target delivery date, the runtime you need and any reference material so the first reply can carry a real quote instead of a question.

## What is not published

No personal email address, phone number or street address appears on this site. Details a contract needs are exchanged only through the platform's own process once a commission is agreed.

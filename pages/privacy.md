This page states what the journal does not collect, what the hosting and advertising layers can record, and how to turn personalised advertising off. Effective 18 November 2026.

## The short version

There are no accounts, no comment form and no newsletter, and the site never asks for a name, email address or phone number. There is no first-party analytics script.

The host (GitHub Pages) keeps standard request logs while serving pages, and **Google AdSense and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.** Section 3 explains how to opt out.

## 1. What this journal collects directly

Nothing. The site has no sign-up, login, account, comment form, guestbook, newsletter, contact form, analytics script, tracking pixel or session recording.

Messages arrive through the external channels listed on the [contact page](../contact/) — a marketplace storefront and a shop platform. Anything left there is governed by that service's policy; this journal does not copy it into a separate database.

## 2. Hosting logs

The journal is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request data — IP address, browser information, time of request — as part of delivering a page. That logging belongs to the host, not to this journal, and is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 3. Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense and third-party vendors)

This journal may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's **prior visits to this website or other websites**.

- Google's use of advertising cookies, including the **DoubleClick cookie**, enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- How Google uses data in advertising is described in [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- The journal does not pass any visitor's name, email address or phone number to an advertising vendor, and exchanges no personal information with vendors directly.

### How to opt out of personalised advertising

You can opt out at any time, from either of these:

- **Google Ads Settings** — [https://adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com). Personalised ads can be turned off without signing in.
- **Industry opt-out page** — [https://www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). Opt out of participating vendors' personalised advertising in one step.

Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised, so they are no longer based on your browsing history. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA and the UK, Google's consent management tool may ask for consent before advertising cookies are used.

## 4. Affiliate links

Articles contain affiliate links. Following one lets the destination site (Gumroad, ElevenLabs, or a marketplace) set its own cookie to attribute a purchase or an enquiry. Those cookies and the data behind them are handled under that site's policy. Which links are affiliate links is stated on the [disclosure page](../disclosure/).

## 5. External links

The journal links out to other websites. Their content and their handling of personal data are their responsibility, and this policy does not apply to them.

## 6. Children

The journal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. Since no personal information is collected at all, none is stored.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Because the journal holds no personal data of its own, there is nothing here to delete. For advertising cookies, use the opt-outs in section 3; for hosting logs, contact GitHub. For a message sent through an external channel, deletion has to be requested from that service.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If the actual handling of data changes — a different advertising vendor, a new tool — this page is updated first and the effective date changes with it.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy go through the channels on the [contact page](../contact/). No personal email address or phone number is published.
